# Privacy Policy – Song Chain Antakshari

**Effective Date:** October 20, 2026  
**Website:** [https://github.com/greenskyedevs](https://greenskyedevs.github.io/songchain-privacy-policy/)

## 1. Introduction and Who We Are

Welcome to **Song Chain Antakshari**, also known as **Song Chain**, an offline Antakshari-style party game for Android.

The app is developed by **greenskyedevs**, based in India. This Privacy Policy explains what information is stored by the app, what information may be collected by third-party services, how that information is used, and what choices and rights you have regarding your privacy.

Song Chain is designed to work offline for gameplay. It does not require an account, sign-up, or login. The app does not record audio, use your microphone or camera, access your contacts or location, or display song titles or lyrics.

By using the app, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. Where applicable law requires consent for specific data processing, such as personalised advertising, we will seek consent as described below.

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Information Stored on Your Device

Song Chain stores certain information locally on your Android device to support gameplay and remember your preferences. This includes:

- **Settings:** Your sound, music, haptics, and animation preferences.
- **Preferences:** Your favourite languages and challenges, and items you choose to hide.
- **Custom games:** Titles, selected items, and words you enter when creating custom games.
- **Game results:** Counts of songs sung, passes, and game duration, used to display your results screen.
- **Pro purchase status:** A cached record of whether you have purchased Pro, your plan type, and its expiry date, where applicable.

This information is stored in the app's local database. The developer does not receive or upload this locally stored gameplay information.

Your Android device's standard Auto Backup feature may include app data in your own Google account backup, depending on your Android settings. This backup is controlled by Google and your device settings, and the developer cannot access it.

### B. Information Collected by Third Parties

Song Chain integrates the following third-party services for advertising and purchase management.

**Google AdMob**

Free users may see advertisements served by Google AdMob. Pro users do not see ads.

AdMob may collect or process the following information:

- Android advertising ID.
- IP address.
- Device and app information.
- Advertising interaction data.

This information may be used to serve advertisements, limit how frequently ads appear, measure advertising performance, and prevent fraud. Advertisements may be personalised unless you opt out or applicable consent requirements prevent personalisation.

**Google Play Billing**

Google Play processes payments for Song Chain Pro purchases. Payment information, such as card, UPI, and bank details, is handled by Google Play. The developer does not receive these payment details.

**RevenueCat**

RevenueCat helps verify purchases and restore Pro access after reinstalling the app or moving to a new phone.

RevenueCat receives information that may include:

- A random, anonymous app user ID generated by the app, which is not linked by the app to your name or email address.
- Purchase and transaction records and receipts from Google Play.
- Basic device and app information, such as app version, operating system version, and country or store region.

These services process information according to their respective privacy policies.

The app does not include analytics SDKs or crash-reporting SDKs, and it does not sell personal data or share data with data brokers.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information is used for the following purposes:

- Remembering your game settings and preferences.
- Saving custom games and displaying completed-game results.
- Checking and maintaining your Pro purchase status.
- Restoring purchases after reinstalling the app or changing devices.
- Displaying advertisements to free users.
- Managing advertising frequency and measuring ad performance.
- Preventing fraudulent purchases and advertising activity.
- Meeting applicable legal obligations.

The developer does not use the locally stored gameplay information for advertising or upload it to the developer's servers.

## 4. Advertising and Your Choices

Free users may receive the following advertisements through Google AdMob:

- Full-screen interstitial advertisements, shown at most every second game when leaving the results screen.
- Optional rewarded video advertisements that users choose to watch to unlock a game mode once.

Song Chain does not display banner advertisements. Pro users see no advertisements.

Advertising content is restricted to a **PG, family-friendly rating**.

Where required, including for users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the app presents Google's User Messaging Platform consent form before personalised advertising. You can change your advertising consent choice in the app under **Settings → Ad privacy options**.

You can also reset or delete your Android advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation through **Android Settings → Google → Ads (Privacy → Ads)**. The exact menu labels may vary by Android version or device.

Changing these settings does not necessarily stop all advertisements. It may change how advertisements are personalised.

For more information, see [Google's Advertising Policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads) and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 5. Purchases and Subscriptions

Song Chain offers **Song Chain Pro**, which unlocks all game modes and custom games and removes advertisements.

Available purchase options are:

- **Six-month subscription:** ₹99, automatically renewing.
- **Lifetime purchase:** ₹199, a one-time payment.

Google Play processes all payments. The developer does not receive your card, UPI, or bank account details.

The six-month subscription renews automatically until you cancel it. You can manage or cancel it through **Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions**.

Uninstalling Song Chain does not cancel your subscription. You must cancel it through Google Play to prevent future renewals.

Refunds are subject to [Google Play's refund policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637).

RevenueCat helps verify purchases and restore Pro access when you reinstall the app or use a new phone signed in to the same Google account.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

**Locally stored data:** Your local settings, preferences, custom games, and game results are deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall Song Chain. Data included in an Android Auto Backup may remain in your Google account backup, depending on your settings.

**RevenueCat purchase records:** If you want to request deletion of information associated with your anonymous RevenueCat app user ID, contact us at greenskyedevs@gmail.com. We will review the request and handle it subject to applicable legal requirements and any records that must be retained.

Deleting purchase records may affect purchase verification or restoration. It does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription or guarantee deletion of records held by Google.

**Google and AdMob information:** Information collected by Google or AdMob is subject to Google's applicable retention and deletion policies. You can review Google's privacy information and available controls using the links in Section 6.

You should keep your device secure and use the privacy controls available through Android and Google Play.
